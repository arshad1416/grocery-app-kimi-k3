1. Quick Summary

StopHop is a privacy-first grocery list app. Your grocery lists are stored locally on your device and encrypted with military-grade encryption (XChaCha20-Poly1305). We don't run central servers that see your data. When you use family sharing, your lists sync through a self-hosted relay server that you control — or through our optional managed relay if you prefer not to self-host. Either way, your data is end-to-end encrypted and we cannot read it.

2. What We Collect

StopHop collects as little as possible:

  • Grocery list data — item names, quantities, categories, notes. Stored locally in an encrypted database on your device.
  • Device identifier — a randomly generated ID used for family pairing. Not linked to your identity.
  • Family member display names — names you choose for yourself within the app. Encrypted at rest.
  • Crash diagnostics — if you leave crash reporting enabled (on by default), anonymous crash data is sent to Sentry. See Crash Reporting below.

We do not collect: your real name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, advertising identifiers, or any browsing history.

3. How Your Data Is Stored

  • On-device: All grocery data lives in a WatermelonDB database, encrypted with XChaCha20-Poly1305. Sensitive keys (encryption keys, pairing codes) are stored in your device's secure enclave via expo-secure-store.
  • In transit: All network communication (family sync, relay connections) uses encrypted WebSocket connections. Sync payloads are end-to-end encrypted — the relay server only forwards opaque ciphertext.
  • Self-hosted relay: If you run your own relay server, your data never leaves infrastructure you control. The relay stores no persistent data — it only relays encrypted messages between your paired devices.
  • Managed relay: If you use the managed tier, encrypted sync payloads transit our relay. We cannot decrypt them. We do not log, store, or analyze the contents of your lists.

4. Family Sharing & Relay

StopHop's family sharing works by pairing devices via QR code. Paired devices sync through a WebSocket relay server. In self-hosted mode, you run this relay yourself. In managed mode, our relay forwards encrypted messages only.

The relay server sees only: your device ID (a random string), connection timestamps, and encrypted blobs it cannot read. No grocery item names, list contents, or personal information are visible to the relay.

5. Optional Features

Price Lookups (Opt-In)

If you enable the price lookup feature, normalized and hashed item names may be sent to third-party price sources (crowd-sourced pool, Instacart, or web scraping on self-hosted mode) to fetch approximate prices. Item names are one-way hashed before transmission — price sources see a hash, not your actual item names. Your shopping habits are not tracked or stored. You can disable this at any time in Settings.

Flyer Scanning (Opt-In)

If you photograph a store flyer to extract prices, the photo is stripped of location and camera metadata (EXIF) on your device and then sent to your configured relay server for AI extraction, after which it is discarded. Unlike your grocery lists, flyer photos are not end-to-end encrypted — the relay can see the flyer image contents (store and product names and prices printed on the flyer). Self-host the relay to keep flyer photos inside your own network. This feature only runs when you tap the flyer-scan camera button and can be disabled in Settings.

Voice Input (Planned)

When enabled, microphone access is used to convert speech to text for adding items. Voice data is processed on-device using your device's speech recognition engine. No audio is recorded or transmitted by StopHop.

Barcode Scanning (Planned)

Camera access is used solely to scan product barcodes for adding items. No images are captured, stored, or transmitted. Camera frames are processed in real-time and discarded immediately.

Push Notifications

StopHop uses expo-notifications for local reminders (e.g., shopping reminders). Notification data stays on your device. We do not send remote push notifications through any third-party service.

6. Crash Reporting

StopHop uses Sentry for anonymous crash reporting. When a crash occurs, the following may be sent:

  • Device model and OS version
  • App version
  • Stack trace (code-level error information)
  • Anonymous device identifier (not linked to you personally)

Crash reports do not contain grocery item names, list contents, or any personal information. You can disable crash reporting in Settings → Privacy → Crash Reporting within the app.

7. Your Rights & Data Deletion

Because StopHop stores data locally on your device:

  • To delete your data: Simply uninstall the app, or use the "Clear Local Prices" button in Settings to remove price data.
  • To opt out of crash reporting: Toggle off "Crash Reporting" in Settings → Privacy.
  • To leave a family: Unpair your device — all synced data is removed from the relay automatically.
  • To export your data: Your grocery data is in a local SQLite database. We plan to add a CSV export feature in a future update.

For managed relay users, you may request deletion of any server-side data by contacting us at privacy@groceryapp.app. Since we only store encrypted blobs relayed between your devices, deletion is effectively immediate upon relay disconnection.

8. Children's Privacy

StopHop is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will notify you of material changes by updating the "Last updated" date and, where appropriate, through an in-app notification. Continued use of StopHop after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
